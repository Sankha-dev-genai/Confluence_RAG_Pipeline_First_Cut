The following [outline](/wiki/Outline_(list) "Outline (list)") is provided as an overview of, and topical guide to, machine learning:

**Machine learning** (**ML**) is a subfield of [artificial intelligence](/wiki/Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence") within [computer science](/wiki/Computer_science "Computer science") that evolved from the study of [pattern recognition](/wiki/Pattern_recognition "Pattern recognition") and [computational learning theory](/wiki/Computational_learning_theory "Computational learning theory").[[1]](#cite_note-Britannica-1) In 1959, [Arthur Samuel](/wiki/Arthur_Samuel_(computer_scientist) "Arthur Samuel (computer scientist)") defined machine learning as a "field of study that gives computers the ability to learn without being explicitly programmed".[[2]](#cite_note-arthur_samuel_machine_learning_def-2) ML involves the study and construction of [algorithms](/wiki/Algorithm "Algorithm") that can [learn](/wiki/Learning "Learning") from and make predictions on [data](/wiki/Data "Data").[[3]](#cite_note-3) These algorithms operate by building a [model](/wiki/Mathematical_model "Mathematical model") from a [training set](/wiki/Training,_validation,_and_test_data_sets "Training, validation, and test data sets") of example observations to make data-driven predictions or decisions expressed as outputs, rather than following strictly static program instructions.

## How can machine learning be categorized?

- An [academic discipline](/wiki/Academic_discipline "Academic discipline")
- A branch of [science](/wiki/Science "Science")
  - An [applied science](/wiki/Applied_science "Applied science")
    - A subfield of [computer science](/wiki/Computer_science "Computer science")
      - A branch of [artificial intelligence](/wiki/Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence")
      - A subfield of [soft computing](/wiki/Soft_computing "Soft computing")
      - Application of [statistics](/wiki/Statistics "Statistics")

### Paradigms of machine learning

- [Supervised learning](/wiki/Supervised_learning "Supervised learning"), where the model is trained on labeled data
- [Unsupervised learning](/wiki/Unsupervised_learning "Unsupervised learning"), where the model tries to identify patterns in unlabeled data
- [Reinforcement learning](/wiki/Reinforcement_learning "Reinforcement learning"), where the model learns to make decisions by receiving rewards or penalties.
