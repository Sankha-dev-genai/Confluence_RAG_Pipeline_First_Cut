**Ab Initio Software** is an American [software](/wiki/Enterprise_software "Enterprise software") corporation based in [Lexington, Massachusetts](/wiki/Lexington,_Massachusetts "Lexington, Massachusetts"). The company specializes in developing software for managing information processing systems.[[1]](#cite_note-1) It was founded in 1995 by the former CEO of [Thinking Machines Corporation](/wiki/Thinking_Machines_Corporation "Thinking Machines Corporation"), [Sheryl Handler](/wiki/Sheryl_Handler "Sheryl Handler"), and several other former employees after the bankruptcy of that company.[[2]](#cite_note-2)
