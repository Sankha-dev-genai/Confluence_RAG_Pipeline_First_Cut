**Data build tool** (**dbt**) is an [open-source](/wiki/Open-source_software "Open-source software") command line tool that helps analysts and engineers [transform](/wiki/Data_transformation "Data transformation") data in their [warehouse](/wiki/Data_warehouse "Data warehouse") more effectively.[[2]](#cite_note-2)

## History

It started at [RJMetrics](/wiki/RJMetrics "RJMetrics") in 2016 as a solution to add basic transformation capabilities to Stitch (acquired by Talend in 2018).[[3]](#cite_note-3) The earliest versions of dbt allowed analysts to contribute to the data transformation process following the best practices of software engineering.[[4]](#cite_note-4)

From the beginning, dbt was open source.[[5]](#cite_note-5) In 2018, the dbt Labs team (then called Fishtown Analytics) released a commercial product on top of dbt Core.[[6]](#cite_note-6)

In January 2025, dbt Labs acquired SDF Labs, a Seattle-based startup developing SQL compilation and static analysis technology for analytics engineering.[[7]](#cite_note-7) SDF Labs was founded in 2022 by Lukas Schulte, Wolfram Schulte, Elias DeFaria, and Michael Levin.[[8]](#cite_note-8)

## Funding

In April 2020, dbt Labs announced its Series A led by [Andreessen Horowitz](/wiki/Andreessen_Horowitz "Andreessen Horowitz").[[9]](#cite_note-9) In November, dbt Labs announced its Series B led by Andreessen Horowitz and [Sequoia](/wiki/Sequoia_Capital "Sequoia Capital").[[10]](#cite_note-10) And in June 2021, dbt Labs raised its Series C led by [Altimeter](/wiki/Altimeter_Capital "Altimeter Capital"), Sequoia, and Andreessen Horowitz.[[11]](#cite_note-11) In February 2022, the company raised $222 million for its Series D, at a $4.2 billion valuation.[[12]](#cite_note-12) In October 2025, dbt Labs announced a definitive agreement to merge with Fivetran in an all-stock deal, with Fivetran CEO George Fraser to lead the combined company and dbt Labs founder Tristan Handy serving as president; the merger was pending regulatory approval as of 2026, with the combined entity projected to approach $600 million in annual recurring revenue.[[13]](#cite_note-fivetran-dbt-merger-13)

## Overview

Dbt enables analytics engineers to transform data in their warehouses by writing select statements, and turns these select statements into tables and views. Dbt does the transformation (T) in [extract, load, transform](/wiki/Extract,_load,_transform "Extract, load, transform") (ELT) processes – it does not extract or load data, but is designed to be performant at transforming data already inside of a warehouse. Dbt has the goal of allowing analysts to work more like software engineers, in line with the dbt viewpoint.[[14]](#cite_note-14)

Dbt uses [YAML](/wiki/YAML "YAML") files to declare properties. `seed` is a type of [reference table](/wiki/Reference_table "Reference table") used in dbt for static or infrequently changed data, like for example [country codes](/wiki/List_of_ISO_3166_country_codes "List of ISO 3166 country codes") or [lookup tables](/wiki/Lookup_table "Lookup table")), which are [CSV](/wiki/Comma_separated_values "Comma separated values") based and typically stored in a seeds [folder](/wiki/Directory_(computing) "Directory (computing)").
