**Aster Data Systems** was a [data management](/wiki/Data_management "Data management") and [analysis](/wiki/Analytics "Analytics") software company headquartered in [San Carlos, California](/wiki/San_Carlos,_California "San Carlos, California"). It was founded in 2005 and acquired by [Teradata](/wiki/Teradata "Teradata") in 2011.

## History

Aster Data was co-founded in 2005 by [Stanford University](/wiki/Stanford_University "Stanford University") graduate students George Candea, Mayank Bawa, and Tasso Argyros.[[1]](#cite_note-1)[[2]](#cite_note-close-2) It received funding from [First Round Capital](/wiki/First_Round_Capital "First Round Capital"), [Sequoia Capital](/wiki/Sequoia_Capital "Sequoia Capital"), [Institutional Venture Partners](/wiki/Institutional_Venture_Partners "Institutional Venture Partners"), Cambrian Ventures, [Jafco Ventures](/wiki/Jafco_Ventures "Jafco Ventures") as well as [angel investors](/wiki/Angel_investor "Angel investor"), including [Rajeev Motwani](/wiki/Rajeev_Motwani "Rajeev Motwani"), [Ron Conway](/wiki/Ron_Conway "Ron Conway") and [David Cheriton](/wiki/David_Cheriton "David Cheriton").[[3]](#cite_note-3)
It received its first round of funding of $5 million in 2005, a second round of $17 million in February 2009, and third round of $30 million in September 2010.[[4]](#cite_note-crunch-4)

Aster was mentioned in 2010 by [Intelligent Enterprise](/wiki/Intelligent_Enterprise_(magazine) "Intelligent Enterprise (magazine)")' editor.[[5]](#cite_note-5)[[6]](#cite_note-6)
It was ranked seventh in 2011 of venture-funded companies by *[The Wall Street Journal](/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal "The Wall Street Journal")*.[[7]](#cite_note-7)
Argyros (Chief Technical Officer at the time) was listed as a technology pioneer of information technology and new media by the [World Economic Forum](/wiki/World_Economic_Forum "World Economic Forum") in 2011.[[8]](#cite_note-8)

[Teradata](/wiki/Teradata "Teradata") had acquired an 11 percent ownership interest in Aster Data Systems in September 2010.
On March 3, 2011, Teradata agreed to pay an additional $263 million for the remaining ownership interest, net of debt and other expenses.[[9]](#cite_note-9)[[10]](#cite_note-10)
The acquisition completed in April 2011.[[2]](#cite_note-close-2)
In September 2011 a [computer appliance](/wiki/Computer_appliance "Computer appliance") version of the product was announced, with pre-configured software bundled with hardware.[[11]](#cite_note-11)
Aster Data hosted a "Data Analytics Summit" trade show through 2012, made up of regional events.[[12]](#cite_note-12)

In October 2012, Aster announced a second version of its appliance. In addition to the Aster database software, another appliance was available with nodes running the [Hortonworks](/wiki/Hortonworks "Hortonworks") distribution of [Apache Hadoop](/wiki/Apache_Hadoop "Apache Hadoop").[[13]](#cite_note-13)[[14]](#cite_note-14)

In October 2013, version 6 of Aster database software was announced. It supported [graph database](/wiki/Graph_database "Graph database") technology, and a file system that the company said was compatible with the Hadoop distributed file system.[[15]](#cite_note-15)[[16]](#cite_note-16)
After Bawa left the company in 2014, he was named a young achiever by the [Indian Institute of Technology Bombay](/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Bombay "Indian Institute of Technology Bombay") (from which he graduated in 1999).[[17]](#cite_note-17)

In June 2014, Teradata announced a product using the [R programming language](/wiki/R_(programming_language) "R (programming language)").[[18]](#cite_note-18)
In February 2015, Teradata announced an "AppCenter" using Aster technology.[[19]](#cite_note-19)
In October, 2015 Teradata announced a set of analytics techniques and applications to run on [Apache Hadoop](/wiki/Apache_Hadoop "Apache Hadoop"), marketed for the [Internet of things](/wiki/Internet_of_things "Internet of things").[[20]](#cite_note-20) In 2016, Aster Analytics was made available on Amazon AWS Marketplace for self-service, DIY customers. It became available at Microsoft Azure Marketplace in 2017.
