**Apatar** is an open source [ETL (Extract-Transform-Load)](/wiki/Extract,_transform,_load "Extract, transform, load") and data integration software application.

## History

Apatar open source project was founded in 2005.[[1]](#cite_note-1) The first version of the tool was released under the GPLv2 license at www.sourceforge.net in February 2007. In April 2007, Apatar alpha version was demonstrated to its strategic partners, including [MySQL](/wiki/MySQL "MySQL") and BlackDuck. Apatar, Inc.,[[2]](#cite_note-2) a commercial company that provides support for the Apatar open source software, was founded in 2007 [[3]](#cite_note-3) as a branch of [Altoros](/wiki/Altoros "Altoros").

## Products

The company’s main product is **Apatar**,[[4]](#cite_note-mashup-4)[[5]](#cite_note-apatar_explained-5) a cross-platform open source desktop data integration tool that provides connectivity to a variety of databases, applications, protocols, files, and many more.

## Users and customers

Apatar’s user and customer base ranges from small companies and individuals to large organizations such as the [World Bank Group](/wiki/World_Bank_Group "World Bank Group"), [Thomson Reuters](/wiki/Thomson_Reuters "Thomson Reuters"), [John Wiley & Sons](/wiki/John_Wiley_%26_Sons "John Wiley & Sons"), [R.R. Donnelley](/wiki/RR_Donnelley "RR Donnelley"), [Autodesk](/wiki/Autodesk "Autodesk").[[6]](#cite_note-6)[[7]](#cite_note-7) and more.
