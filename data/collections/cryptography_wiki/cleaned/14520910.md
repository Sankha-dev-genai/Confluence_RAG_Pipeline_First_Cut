**Blacker** (styled **BLACKER**) is a [U.S. Department of Defense](/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Defense "United States Department of Defense") computer [network security](/wiki/Network_security "Network security") project designed to achieve [A1 class](/wiki/Trusted_Computer_System_Evaluation_Criteria "Trusted Computer System Evaluation Criteria") ratings (very high assurance) of the Trusted Computer System Evaluation Criteria ([TCSEC](/wiki/TCSEC "TCSEC")).[[1]](#cite_note-1)[[2]](#cite_note-2)

The first Blacker program began in the late 1970s, with a follow-on eventually producing fielded devices in the late 1980s.[[3]](#cite_note-3) It was the first secure system with trusted [end-to-end encryption](/wiki/End-to-end_encryption "End-to-end encryption") on the United States' [Defense Data Network](/wiki/Defense_Data_Network "Defense Data Network").[[4]](#cite_note-4)

The project was implemented by [SDC](/wiki/System_Development_Corporation "System Development Corporation") (software), and [Burroughs](/wiki/Burroughs_Corporation "Burroughs Corporation") (hardware), and after their merger, by the resultant company [Unisys](/wiki/Unisys "Unisys").[[5]](#cite_note-5)
