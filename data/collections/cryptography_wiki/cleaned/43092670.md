**Batch cryptography** is a field of [cryptology](/wiki/Cryptology "Cryptology") focused on the design of [cryptographic protocols](/wiki/Cryptographic_protocol "Cryptographic protocol") that perform operations—such as [encryption](/wiki/Encryption "Encryption"), decryption, [key exchange](/wiki/Key_exchange "Key exchange"), and [authentication](/wiki/Authentication "Authentication")—on multiple inputs simultaneously, rather than processing each input individually.[[1]](#cite_note-1) Batching cryptographic operations can significantly reduce the marginal cost of handling individual inputs—a principle that was first introduced by Amos Fiat in 1989.[[2]](#cite_note-2)
