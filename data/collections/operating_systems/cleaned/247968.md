This article provides a brief [timeline](/wiki/Chronology "Chronology") for the evolution of [operating systems](/wiki/Operating_system "Operating system"). For a narrative explaining the overall developments, see the [History of operating systems](/wiki/History_of_operating_systems "History of operating systems").

## 1940s
